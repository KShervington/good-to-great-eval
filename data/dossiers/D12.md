# Subject dossier: Bellmoor 250

**As of:** fourteen months before opening day.
**Subject type:** Civic initiative (commemorative festival)

*This dossier contains the executive director's covering note asking for a review, followed by the minutes of the commission's last two steering-committee meetings.*

---

## Covering note from the executive director

Our chair has read *Good to Great* twice and keeps a copy on the table at every meeting. She wants Bellmoor 250 to be "not just good, but great," and she asked me to have it run through the framework properly: who's on the bus, our hedgehog, our flywheel, what to stop doing. We have real people, a real budget and real stakes, so please treat us like any other organization. Some background:

- **Bellmoor 250** is the town's commemoration of the 250th anniversary of its charter: **ten days in June of next year**, with an opening parade, a tall-ship visit to the harbor, an outdoor historical pageant with a cast of 300, a newly commissioned symphonic work, a time capsule and a fireworks finale.
- It is run by the **Bellmoor Quarter-Millennium Commission**, chartered by the town council two years ago. Under its charter, the commission dissolves ninety days after its final audit is accepted. All four staff, including me, are on fixed-term contracts that end with it. I am a contracted event producer; this is my 26-month engagement.
- **Budget: $1,362,500.** Town appropriation $500,000; state heritage grant $240,000; sponsorships pledged $431,000; ticketed events (pageant, symphony) projected at $121,500; merchandise projected at $70,000.
- **People:** a 15-member commission chaired by a retired judge; nine subcommittees; **410 volunteers** signed up so far (target 600).
- **Targets the commission adopted:** 180,000 visits over ten days; "regional media impressions" of 25 million; "a lasting legacy for Bellmoor."

## Steering committee minutes, meeting 1 (summary)

**Present:** chair, ED, treasurer, chairs of the Program, Sponsorship, Volunteers, Legacy and Youth subcommittees.

1. **Sponsorship report.** Pledges total $431,000 from 11 sponsors. The lead sponsor, **Calder Mills**, has pledged **$270,000**, payable in two installments: $110,000 (received) and **$160,000 due in April**. The treasurer noted that the town's cash-flow loan covers deposits for the tall ship and fireworks only until May.
2. **Program.** The symphony commission is on schedule; the composer has delivered the first two movements. The tall-ship organization requires its final deposit by March.
3. **Youth subcommittee proposal: the Bellmoor 250 app.** An augmented-reality walking tour that overlays historical scenes on today's streets, plus a festival schedule and ticketing. Quote: **$96,000** from a local studio. A regional tech company has offered to cover $20,000 of it. The youth chair presented a survey of 640 high-school students in which 72% said they would use it. Supporters argue that it would be the most visible legacy piece and would reach families who won't attend the pageant. The treasurer pointed out it is not in the budget. Referred to the next meeting.
4. **Legacy.** The oral-history project has recorded 141 of a planned 240 interviews. The library has agreed to host the archive permanently.

## Steering committee minutes, meeting 2 (summary)

1. **Volunteer appreciation.** The committee thanked Ed Farrow, who retires this month after 31 years as community-relations manager at Calder Mills and who brought in the Calder pledge. Calder has not yet named his successor.
2. **Press clippings** (circulated by the communications volunteer, for information): the regional paper reports that Calder Mills' parent company will "consolidate production capacity" at its southern plant over the next 18 months and that its Bellmoor operations "are under review." Coverage of the tall-ship announcement reached 1.4 million impressions.
3. **Attendance planning.** The Program chair asked how the 180,000 target was set. The ED explained that it came from the commission's first workshop. The town's population is 31,400. No attendance records survive from the town's 200th anniversary; the closest comparison, a neighboring city's bicentennial nine years ago, reported "over 100,000" attendees but did not publish a method.
4. **App proposal.** Deferred again. The youth chair asked for a decision by January so the studio can start.
5. **"Bellmoor Days."** A town councillor attending as a guest proposed that the commission "turn 250 into an annual festival" using the staging, the volunteer list and the sponsor relationships. The chair noted that the charter does not allow the commission to continue beyond its dissolution, and that any annual event would have to be created and funded separately by the council.
6. **Next meeting:** the chair asked members to read the chapter on the Hedgehog Concept beforehand.
