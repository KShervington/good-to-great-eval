# Subject dossier: Delivery Platform team, Brightwater Financial

**As of:** the end of the third quarter of Year 0.
**Subject type:** Team inside a larger organization (internal engineering platform team)

*Context, added by the person who compiled this dossier:* Brightwater Financial is a consumer payments and lending company with about 900 employees, about 150 of them software engineers. The Delivery Platform team (10 engineers, one engineering manager, one technical program manager) builds and runs **Runway**, the company's standard way to build, test and deploy a service: service templates, CI pipelines and progressive rollout. Services not on Runway use older pipelines that each product org maintains itself. The team has existed for three years. Below are the team's own quarterly retrospective notes, unedited apart from removing names of people outside the team.

### What went well

### What didn't go well
- **Fast rollback (< 5 min) epic slipped again.** It was deprioritized behind template v3 and the Kotlin starter. This is the third quarter running.
- In the architecture review, the Payments platform lead repeated that Payments will move its services to Runway "once rollback is under five minutes and you support our change-approval step." (Payments has a manual sign-off step for its card-network compliance audit.)
- Too many interrupts from teams asking for one-off pipeline tweaks.
- The build cluster's disk alerts are noisy. Bartek muted two of them; we need a proper fix.

### Discussion: Atlas
The VP of Engineering has offered **two additional headcount** if the team builds and owns **Atlas**, an internal developer portal: a service catalog, ownership directory, docs search and scorecards.
- Evidence the VP shared: in the developer survey, **58%** of engineers agreed that "finding the owner or docs for a service is hard." At a peer company she visited, a similar portal cut new-engineer onboarding from about six weeks to about three.
- Most of the team is excited. It is greenfield work, highly visible, and would make Runway's templates easier to discover.
- Two people asked whether Atlas would delay the rollback epic again. Ines said she would rather the team decide together and asked everyone to think about it before the planning meeting.
- Ines also noted that the VP wants an answer before the Q4 planning offsite.
