# Subject dossier: Heart Failure Transitions Program, St. Aldric Regional Medical Center

**As of:** early in Year 1, covering results for Year 0.
**Subject type:** Team inside a larger organization (hospital clinical team)

*This dossier is the program manager's annual memo to the Vice President of Nursing, with two attachments. Patient identifiers were removed before it was shared.*

---

**To:** VP of Nursing
**From:** Colette Marsh, RN, MSN, Manager, Heart Failure Transitions Program (HFTP)
**Re:** Year 0 annual summary and Year 1 priorities

### 1. Who we are
HFTP was launched four years ago to help heart-failure patients make it home and stay home after discharge. St. Aldric is a 418-bed regional hospital. We are 11.6 FTE: five nurse navigators, two pharmacists (1.6 FTE), two social workers, two community health workers and me. Our Year 0 operating budget was **$1.46M**, fully funded from the hospital's operating budget.

Our protocol: a bedside enrollment visit before discharge; a nurse call within 48 hours; a clinic or home visit within 7 days; a pharmacist medication call around day 10; a community health worker home visit by day 14; and a "graduation" call at day 30.

### 2. Year 0 results
| Measure | Enrolled | Not enrolled (HF) |
|---|---|---|
| Heart-failure discharges | 1,146 | 556 |
| 30-day all-cause readmission | **15.9%** | 21.8% |
| Follow-up visit within 7 days | 82% | 46% |
| "Would recommend" (patient survey, n = 391) | 94% | n/a |

Enrollment is offered to every heart-failure patient discharged home. Patients who decline, cannot be reached by phone, or are discharged to a nursing facility are not enrolled. Our enrollment rate rose from 61% to 67% this year.

The hospital's overall 30-day heart-failure readmission rate was 17.8%, the best in our state hospital association's peer group for the second straight year. The federal readmissions penalty applied to the hospital for the last fiscal year was 0.71% of base payments, down from 1.12%.

### 3. Stories that matter
Mr. D., 81, was readmitted four times in the year before he met us. Our pharmacist found he was taking two diuretics prescribed by two different specialists. He has not been readmitted in eleven months. His daughter wrote to the CEO.

Our community health workers delivered 214 bathroom scales donated by a local service club, so that patients can track daily weights.

### 4. Year 1 priorities
1. **Raise enrollment to 75%.** We will add a weekend enrollment shift.
2. **Respond to the CMO's request.** The Chief Medical Officer has asked HFTP to lead the hospital's launch of **Hospital at Home**, which provides acute inpatient-level care in patients' homes for selected conditions. The plan would add 6 FTE to our team. I would become director of a combined "Transitions and Home-Based Care" department. A vendor would supply the remote monitoring platform. The CMO points to a published study from another health system that found 19% lower costs and fewer readmissions with this model. The board's quality committee is enthusiastic. I believe our nurses' home-visit experience makes us the natural team for it.
3. **Show our value.** You asked me last quarter what HFTP returns to the hospital. Finance has not yet assigned a cost per readmission for our service line. I am working with them, and I hope to have something for the budget cycle.

### 5. Staffing
Turnover was low: one navigator left to relocate. Pharmacist coverage is thin on Fridays. Our social worker, Ana, completed her clinical licensure.

Thank you for your continued support.
