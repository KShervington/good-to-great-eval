# Subject dossier: Northfield-Brandt Corporation

**As of:** early in Year 0, shortly after the close of the last fiscal year. Everything below was known or knowable on that date. Nothing after it is included.
**Subject type:** Business (publicly listed consumer and paper products manufacturer)

## Overview
Northfield-Brandt is a paper-products company that is more than a century old. About 20 years ago its current chief executive took over. Within a year he sold or closed most of the company's commodity paper mills and a large block of timberland, and he used the cash to compete in branded consumer paper products against **Rival P**, the world's leading packaged-goods marketer. Six years ago he moved the headquarters from the company's original small-town home to a large Sun Belt city.

The company reports three classes of business:
- **Consumer and personal care (the largest):** facial tissue, toilet tissue, towels, disposable diapers, training pants, feminine care, adult incontinence, wipers and health-care products, plus pulp for these products.
- **Paper:** newsprint, printing papers, business and correspondence papers, cigarette and other tobacco-industry papers, and technical papers.
- **Aviation:** aircraft maintenance and services, and a commercial passenger airline.

## Financial position
- Last year net sales were $6.41 billion and net income was $432 million. Stockholders' equity at year-end was $2.26 billion, giving a return on equity of about 19% (calculated).
- Annual returns on the shares have been positive in each of the past six years, and over that span they were well ahead of the broad market.
- Profit by class of business is not available in this dossier.

## Market position
- **Facial tissue:** the company created the category. In the mid-1980s its brand held about half of the market.
- **Diapers:** the premium diaper launched 13 years ago reached about 31% of the market within seven years. Six years ago Rival P sued over a waistband patent, and after nearly two years a federal jury ruled against Rival P. Training pants launched two years ago helped the brand become the best-selling single diaper brand and narrowed Rival P's overall lead.
- **Adult incontinence:** the company's brand is the best-selling retail brand in the country.
- **Europe:** since three years ago the company has been investing heavily in new plants in Europe.
- **Paper:** newsprint and pulp prices follow the commodity cycle.
- **Product experiments:** an anti-viral tissue was test-marketed seven years ago and dropped four years ago because of its high price and limited use.

## The airline
The airline grew out of the corporate flight department, which had run the executives' planes for decades. About 20 years ago the finance staff proposed turning the flight department into a profit center. The company began scheduled passenger service six and a half years ago. In its first full year it had a fatal crash, planes flying about 80% empty, and heavy operating losses. Two years ago it became profitable, flying about two-thirds full. It is now in a $120 million expansion to 15 cities and a fleet of 11 jets.

## Leadership
- **Chairman and chief executive:** 64. He has led the company for 20 years and is a former in-house lawyer, known as reserved and very demanding.
- **President and chief operating officer:** appointed two months ago. He joined 15 years ago and led development of the training pants.

## Strategy and known problems
- **Rival P** is larger, has deeper research and advertising budgets, and leads the U.S. diaper market overall through two brands.
- **The paper businesses** tie up capital in commodity mills that need regular reinvestment.
- **The airline and aviation-services businesses** are growing and profitable. They have nothing in common with consumer products.
- **Questions under discussion:**
  - whether to keep funding the airline's expansion;
  - whether to keep, grow or sell the newsprint, printing-paper and tobacco-paper operations;
  - how fast to keep investing in Europe;
  - how to manage the chief executive succession after 20 years.
