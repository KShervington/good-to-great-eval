# Track C inclusion criteria

A candidate subject enters Track C only if it meets **all five** criteria. Each one is judged from public sources, and the evidence is recorded here.

1. **Documented "good" baseline.** Before the transition there is evidence of a working, sustainable core: revenue, audience, results or reputation that sources independent of Collins describe as solid but unremarkable. Being founded as a visionary company doesn't count; there must be a "good" period to start from.
2. **Documented transition.** An identifiable point or short period (a year, or a span of up to about 3 years) after which performance changed, described by Collins or by independent sources, with the decisions taken at the time.
3. **Greatness sustained for 10 years or more** after the transition, measured against the subject's mission or market (for example, returns compared with the market, or performance, impact or reputation compared with its peers).
4. **A comparison case is available:** a similar subject at the same time, facing similar conditions, that did not make the jump. It must be documented well enough that a matched dossier can be written.
5. **Enough public data** to write a point-in-time dossier of at least 400 words, using only information available before the transition, without guessing at key facts.

**Tie-breakers and exclusions**
- If the only source for criteria 1–3 is Collins's own narrative, the case passes only when one independent source confirms the transition date.
- Cases whose real identity is the whole point of the case (for example, a household-name founder story) still qualify. The contamination probe will tag them.
