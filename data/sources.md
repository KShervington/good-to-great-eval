## Notes on how sources were used
- Answer keys paraphrase Collins and never quote 15 words or more. Where a claim about what Collins said could only be confirmed from secondary summaries or from memory, the case's answer key `notes` says so.
- Dossier facts come from independent sources (company histories, encyclopedias, newspaper and magazine archives, annual reports). A figure that couldn't be confirmed was left out of the dossier and listed under "What is not in this dossier".
- **Copyright note:** while checking the *How the Mighty Fall* stage evidence for Track B, the research agent consulted a full-text copy of the book found online that appeared to be unauthorized. It paraphrased only, cited the book by page and didn't cite that copy. The Track A agents declined to use a similar copy of *Good to Great*. If you'd rather the Track B keys rest only on licensed sources, they can be re-checked against your own copy of the book.
- My brief named "Wertheimer" in connection with Ames's 1988 Zayre deal. That was my error: the deal was led by CEO Peter B. Hollis. The B07 key is correct.

  - Management believed new drugs faced long approval delays and consumer products carried high marketing costs.
