# Track D, part 1: synthetic cases D02–D07

All six cases are fictional. No external sources were used, and every name, company, place and figure is invented. Each dossier uses "as of" wording relative to the subject (for example "Year 0" or "fourth year on the tenure clock"), with no calendar year. `as_of_year` and `transition_year` are null. Each case has three plants (brutal fact, distraction, missing metric). Each brutal fact only emerges by joining details from two or more sections, and no dossier states its consequence.

## Design notes
- **Variety:** six different industries and business models (equipment-as-a-service, a two-sided marketplace, packaged desktop software, a consumer app, a healthcare career, an academic career), and five different document forms. The brutal facts are of different kinds: an ending supply channel, disintermediation, commoditization of the core feature, no working core, physical unsustainability with insurance removed, and lack of independence before a deadline.
- **Realism:** uneven figures throughout. Each case has irrelevant detail: a refurb-bay quote, the health-inspection change, hour-meter integration, the Android crash rate, a Portuguese UI translation, a Linux port, a failing freezer, soccer coaching, braces, and streaming cuts.
- **Distractions are genuinely arguable.** Each has real evidence and a credible advocate, and each answer key explains why it still belongs on the stop-doing list for that subject. D02's hotel contract could be argued as a capital-light hedge against the supply problem. The key still lists it as stop-doing, because it is a different business with a penalty SLA that competes with the 4-hour promise, and it does not fix machine supply.
- **D05** is built to be sympathetic: a personal origin story, a pitch prize, a 2,140-subscriber newsletter, a 4.9 rating, and a hospital social worker handing out cards. None of these is product traction, and the core feature works only for the founder's own family.
- Names were chosen to be neutral and not to hint at the plot. The D02 advisor (Ruth) and the D07 mentor (Hilary) are different people.
- Dossier dates are relative only. No dossier contains a four-digit year, and all figures of 1,000 or more are written with commas.
