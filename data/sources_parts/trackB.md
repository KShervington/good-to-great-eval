# Sources: Track B decline tests (B01–B09)

**Authority for the answer keys.** Jim Collins, *How the Mighty Fall* (HTMF), HarperCollins, 2009. I checked it against a full-text copy and cite it by page. Its tables are:
- Appendix 1, the final study set of fallen cases;
- Appendix 2, the success contrasts;
- Appendix 3, Fannie Mae;
- Appendix 4.A, Stage 2 evidence with dates per company;
- Appendix 4.B, Stage 4 evidence.

Collins is paraphrased throughout. No Collins quotation of 15 words or more is used.

Secondary Collins sources:
- *Good to Great* (2001) for the GTG hedgehogs and transition years (Circuit City 1982, Fannie Mae 1984, Wells Fargo 1983), and for Gault as a Level 4 leader at Rubbermaid.
- *Built to Last* (1994) for Merck's and Motorola's core purposes.
- The GTG hedgehog wording is from recollection of the book and secondary summaries. It is paraphrased, and the judge should score substance.

**Convention for `transition_year` in Track B.** It is the year the decline became visible to outsiders: the first big loss, scandal or bankruptcy. The Good to Great transition year, where one exists, is in the separate field `gtg_transition_year`.
