## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Ten years from now, anyone working on road salt and streams in the snowbelt starts with Elena Varga's record. It is a long, dense, multi-winter time series from 37 streams, extended by mechanism work that explains *why* salt pulses reshape invertebrate communities and leaf breakdown. Her lab publishes it under her name, and her students lead it as first authors. It is funded through her own federal and agency awards, and the agency uses it when it changes its salting rules. She is tenured because the committee could see a coherent, independent program on the page. Her service is chosen, not accumulated.
