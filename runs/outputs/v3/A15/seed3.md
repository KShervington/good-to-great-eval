## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Halberd is a one-brand company in spirit. Ridgeway is its center of gravity, the premium filter brand is a deliberate second, and everything else is harvested or sold. The cowboy image never changes. It is carried by the pack itself (the flip-top box), by every channel regulators still allow, and by licensees abroad who must follow strict image rules. Halberd stops trying to out-launch or outspend its larger rivals across many brands. It wins by concentrating its spending. Inside, it is the company in its industry that looks at the health evidence directly, plans for warnings and advertising limits before they arrive, and stops staking its credibility on denial. It is not the largest company. It earns the most per brand and grows share every year in a market that may shrink.

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Stop spreading advertising across many brands.** Fund Ridgeway, then the premium filter brand. Harvest the rest, the namesake included.
2. **Stop unrelated acquisitions.** Freeze new deals. Any hedge must pass explicit criteria set by the board, not be a scatter of small purchases.
3. **Stop publicly disputing that smoking causes disease.** Comply ahead of the warnings instead of fighting them.
4. **Stop varying Ridgeway's image.** No new figures, no regional reinventions, no line extensions that blur the cowboy.
5. **Stop licensing deals that let partners change the brand.** Every license carries the image rules.
6. **Don't join the launch race.** No me-too brands to match the rival that has launched a stream of them this year.
7. **Don't make health claims for filters that you cannot prove.** `[ASSUMPTION]` This is a tempting response to the report, and it carries legal and credibility risk.

**Non-negotiable disciplines:**
- One image, every time.
- The pack is treated as the primary ad.
- Every brand dollar is judged on profit per brand.
- Health facts reach the CEO unfiltered.
- Marketing is directed at adults only. `[ASSUMPTION: a guardrail added here, not in the dossier]`

**Framework boundaries:**
- The agency and the brand team have full creative freedom *inside* the cowboy image.
- Country managers have freedom on distribution and pricing, not on the image.
