## 1. The great version in one paragraph

The great Harwell-Brandt is a narrower company. It is the world's most trusted maker of everyday self-care brands for the mouth, throat and stomach: the mouthwash, the cough drops, the antacid and effervescent remedies, and possibly breath-related gum. These brands lead their categories in most of the 140 countries where it already operates. Every health claim it makes is backed by evidence before it airs. It grows by taking proven brands into more countries, not by buying unrelated companies. It runs clean plants and clean books in every country. It no longer owns a bakery, an optical business or a distant-second razor line. Pharmaceuticals were decided on once, from an honest pipeline audit, and the decision stuck. It outperforms because it compounds a few great brands year after year, and stops trying to be a consumer company in one year and a drug company the next.

2. **Stop owning the bakery chain.** Start a sale.
3. **Stop owning the optical business.** Start a sale.

5. **Stop announcing strategy before the facts are in.** No third public direction in two years.
6. **Stop making health claims without prior evidence.** Nothing airs until the substantiation function signs off.
7. **Stop any payment practice abroad that would not survive public disclosure,** in all 140 countries, not just the 14 already identified.
8. **Stop funding broad drug research "to keep up."** Hold research spending flat until the pipeline audit is done, then fund only what the audit supports, or exit.

## 7. Core ideology and BHAG

**Core values:**
1. Every claim is true and proven before we make it.
2. No one is harmed making our products.
3. Clean hands in every country.
4. Build brands; don't buy diversification.

**Core purpose:** help people care for everyday ailments with products they can trust.

**BHAG (20-year horizon):** own the #1 self-care brand for the mouth, throat or stomach in every major market we serve.

**Clock-building mechanisms:**
- A claims-substantiation board with veto power over all health claims.
- A capital rule: each brand must reach #1 or #2 in its category and country within a set period `[ASSUMPTION: about 3–5 years]`, or be fixed or sold.
- An acquisition gate: the board checks every proposed deal against the stop-doing list.
- A compliance hotline to the audit committee, plus annual country audits.
- Process-safety audits at every plant.
- An internal CEO succession plan, so the company is not dependent on one founder-style leader again.
