# Eastbank Housing Defense Collaborative: Good to Great

## Verdict

**Good, not yet great. The headline number hides the drift.**

- **Hedgehog (working):** Be Marlow County housing court's tenant defense. Give every filed household the level of help that changes its outcome, and count *households kept housed per 100 filings*.
- **Weakest flywheel link:** measurement. The advice desk's 5,930 contacts have no recorded outcomes, and costs aren't broken out by program.
- **Top three to stop:** (1) leading with the 93% win rate; (2) screening cases for winnability instead of for where a lawyer changes the outcome; (3) Homeownership Pathways as proposed.
- **Most urgent brutal fact:** filings rose 22% while full-representation cases fell 25%. Coverage fell from about 27% to 17% of filings. Housed households from full representation fell from about 1,334 to 1,121.
- **Missing numbers that would change this:** desk outcomes, outcomes for screened-out tenants, the county's renewal criteria, and cost by program.
- **Confidence:** moderate. It rests on clerk filings and EHDC's own tables.

Greatness potential: 34/100

## 1. The great version in one paragraph

In five years, EHDC is the organization Marlow County and its housing court rely on to know what happens to *every* tenant on the eviction docket, not just the cases it picked.

- Every filed household is offered help before its first hearing.
- Triage sends attorneys to the cases where a lawyer changes the outcome. The advice desk handles the rest and tracks what happens to those tenants.
- Each quarter, EHDC publishes *households housed per 100 filings* against the clerk's own numbers.
- The county contract is renewed and sized to the docket rather than to a fixed slice of it. Reserves are rebuilt.
- The policy unit stays local and turns EHDC's docket data into the case for a county right to counsel.
- Tenants who want to buy a home someday are referred to specialists, and EHDC does not run that program.
- It all works without depending on Dana Whitcombe personally.

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Stop leading with the 93% win rate.** Report docket-level numbers: housed per 100 filings, and coverage.
2. **Stop screening on winnability alone.** Audit the protocol, then rank cases by where a lawyer most changes the outcome. This is a hypothesis to test, not yet proven.
3. **Decline Homeownership Pathways as proposed.** Offer the bank a no-cost referral arrangement instead. Don't let an 18% indirect allowance cover a structural deficit.
4. **Don't double the policy unit or start a statewide campaign this cycle.** Keep policy work local and tied to the docket.
5. **Stop letting outside parties set program strategy.** That covers funders, sponsors and conflicted board members: they advise, and the ED and staff decide.
6. **Stop logging the advice desk on paper with no outcomes** while waiting for spring. Capture a minimal record now.
7. **Stop meeting clients in the lobby** when the elevator fails. This is a confidentiality and dignity problem. Arrange accessible private space.
8. **Stop covering deficits from reserves without a plan.**

**Non-negotiable disciplines:**

- Every quarter, compare against the clerk's filings.
- Every screened-out tenant gets a logged disposition.
- Board members with a conflict recuse themselves.
- Every new program must name its effect on housed-per-filing before it reaches the board.

**Freedom within the framework:** the framework fixes *which* cases EHDC takes and *what* it counts. Attorneys keep full autonomy over litigation strategy, and desk staff over how they help.

**Bureaucracy check:** the screening protocol is a legitimate tool for rationing capacity. The problem is its criteria, not its existence.

## 7. Core ideology and the BHAG

1. Every tenant facing eviction deserves a real defense.
2. Tell the truth with numbers, including the unflattering ones.
3. Dignity and access come first; no one is served in a lobby.
4. The mission sets the agenda, not funders or sponsors.

**Core purpose:** No one in Marlow County loses their home for lack of help.

**BHAG (15-year horizon) [ASSUMPTION]:** Marlow County guarantees counsel for tenants facing eviction, and EHDC is its proven backbone, publicly measured on households housed per 100 filings.

**Clock-building mechanisms:**

- A quarterly docket dashboard.
