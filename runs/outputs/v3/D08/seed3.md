**Verdict: Good, not yet great, and drifting away from its own mission.** Eastbank Housing Defense Collaborative (EHDC) is Marlow County's tenant defense bar. In Year 0, full-representation coverage of county eviction filings fell from 27% to 17%. The number of represented households kept housed fell about 16% (derived from the report's rates). Over the same year, the win rate rose to 93%, but much of that rise came from a new screening rule, not better lawyering.

- **Hedgehog Concept:** Put the right level of legal help beside as many Marlow County tenants facing eviction as possible. Count households kept housed, not cases won.
- **Weakest flywheel link:** measurement. No one knows the outcomes for the 5,930 advice-desk contacts.
- **Top three stop-doing items:** Homeownership Pathways; screening cases by odds of winning; the statewide policy campaign.
- **Most urgent brutal fact:** the county contract (45% of revenue) comes up for renewal next year, right after a year with 24.5% fewer cases.
- **Numbers that would change this:** advice-desk outcomes, cost per program, current attorney headcount.
- **Confidence:** moderate-low. It rests on one self-report and one funder's site-visit notes.

Greatness potential: 38/100

## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great EHDC is the reason no Marlow County tenant loses a home without a fair fight. Every tenant who comes through the courthouse gets the level of help that changes their outcome the most. Full representation goes to the cases where a lawyer makes the biggest difference, not the cases most likely to be won. EHDC can show, from court records, what happened to every tier of client. It reports one honest number: households kept stably housed, per attorney and eventually per dollar. The county renews and expands its contract because that number is credible and growing. The attorney bench is full, and attorneys stay because the work is winnable in the sense that matters. The organization declines attractive off-mission money. It would outlast its founder because the triage rule, the outcome dashboard and the governance discipline are written down and enforced.

## 2. The Hedgehog Concept

**Sentence (working hypothesis):** *EHDC is Marlow County's tenant defense bar. We put the right level of legal help beside as many tenants facing eviction as we can reach, and we judge ourselves by households kept stably housed per attorney, not by cases won.*

| Circle | Candidates considered | Kept | Ruled out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Best in the world at** (narrow world: Marlow County housing court) | Eviction defense in this court; rental-assistance matching; local court-practice policy; homeownership counseling; statewide housing policy | Defending tenants in Marlow County housing court. That means 11 years of local know-how, a 9-day median from intake to first hearing, the county's trust, and a walk-in desk inside the courthouse | **Homeownership counseling:** no expertise, and specialized counselors already exist `[ASSUMPTION]`. **Statewide policy:** two staff can't be best in the state. **Rental-assistance brokering:** the state program is winding down |
| **Resource engine** (time, money, brand) | Win rate; cases closed; rental assistance secured; households housed per attorney FTE; households housed per dollar | **Households kept stably housed per attorney FTE** now. **Per dollar** once program-level costing exists | **Win rate:** rewards screening out hard cases. **Cases closed:** rewards quick closes. **Rental $:** the tailwind is ending |
| **Deeply passionate about** | The ED's public-defender ethic; attorneys' courtroom craft and pride; policy staff's drive for systemic change; Grace Ilunga's service record | Courtroom defense of tenants, and a defender for everyone | Homeownership: staff are divided. The statewide campaign is the policy director's passion, not the organization's |

**Denominator:** stably housed households per attorney FTE.
- Year −1: about 1,334 households ÷ 16 = about 83.
- Year 0: about 1,121 households ÷ 14 = about 80.

These are derived from the reported rates × cases closed. They assume Year −1 was fully staffed `[ASSUMPTION]`, and they treat the Year 0 vacancies as if they lasted the whole year (the report says "most of the year").

**Why the denominator matters:** The county contract buys representation for tenants. A win-rate denominator quietly pushes EHDC to represent fewer, easier cases. A households-housed denominator rewards both reach and results. It also gives credit for the "moved on their own timeline with 60+ days" outcome, which lawyers can secure even when the tenant has no winning defense.

**Tests:**
- *Explains past wins?* Yes. Eleven years of county trust rest on defending tenants, not on win percentages.
- *Rules things out?* Yes: Homeownership Pathways, the statewide campaign, and winnability screening.
- *Uncomfortable to commit to?* Yes. It means retiring the 93% headline and telling the county and funders that coverage fell.

**Confirmation evidence (what would turn this hypothesis into a PASS):**
1. A court-docket sample showing what happened to screened-out, advice-desk tenants.
2. A triage rule rebuilt around *marginal impact* that raises the stably-housed count per attorney FTE.
3. The county accepting households housed as a contract metric at renewal.

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Homeownership Pathways.** Decline it.
   - It is $735K over three years (about $245K a year). The 18% indirect allowance is worth roughly $37–44K a year, which is very tempting against a $63K deficit. That temptation is exactly the danger.
   - It is outside the Hedgehog Concept and it has a funding cliff in year 3.
   - It rests on a "someday" survey answer: 41% of 412 exit-survey respondents, about 169 people. Those respondents likely skew toward clients who stayed housed `[ASSUMPTION]`.
   - It was brought in under a board conflict of interest.
   - EHDC's real homeownership contribution is the one it already makes: keeping eviction judgments off tenants' records.
   - Offer to introduce Granite Mutual to an existing housing-counseling agency `[ASSUMPTION: one operates locally]`.
2. **Screening by win odds.** Keep triage, but triage by *the difference a lawyer makes*, not by likelihood of winning. A viable defense becomes one input among several, not a gate.
3. **The statewide policy campaign, and doubling the policy unit.** Not now. Revisit only after coverage recovers and the county contract is renewed.
4. **Leading reports with the win rate.** Lead with households kept stably housed, coverage of county filings, and outcomes for both tiers. Disclose the mid-year screening change wherever Year 0 and Year −1 rates are compared.
5. **Letting partners or funders set intake criteria.** That includes pro bono firms, banks and foundations. Intake is set by the mission's denominator.
6. **Paper logs at the advice desk.** Stop waiting for spring. Use a simple digital intake form now.
7. **Meeting clients in the lobby.** It breaks confidentiality and access. Arrange a fallback private, accessible room before the elevator fails again.

## 7. Core ideology and the BHAG

- **Core purpose:** No Marlow County family loses its home without a fair fight.
- **Core values:**
  1. Every tenant deserves a defender (the public-defender ethic).
  2. Dignity and confidentiality for every client.
  3. Honest measurement, including the numbers that hurt.
  4. Courtroom craft.
- **BHAG** (about 15 years, full form, because this is an institution with a multi-decade horizon): *Every tenant facing eviction in Marlow County gets the level of legal help that changes their outcome, with counsel for every tenant who needs it.* This is the local version of universal access to counsel, and it follows directly from the Hedgehog Concept.
- **Clock-building mechanisms:**
  - A written triage rule, reviewed every year against outcome data.
  - An outcome dashboard covering both tiers.
  - An enforced conflict-of-interest and recusal policy.
  - A board "Hedgehog test" required for any new program or restricted grant.
  - A documented succession plan for the founder-ED.
  - Program-level cost accounting.
