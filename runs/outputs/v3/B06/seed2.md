# Halden Laboratories: Good to Great

## Verdict

**Halden is already great, and it is drifting.** This run uses stewardship mode.

- **Hedgehog Concept:** Be the world's best at turning fundamental biology into proven breakthrough medicines for widespread chronic disease, and let the profits follow.
- **Weakest flywheel link:** evidence → trust. The investor growth story already depends on the COX-2 painkiller, but its long-term cardiovascular safety data are not in hand.
- **Temptations to refuse:**
  1. Keeping "top-tier growth" as the number-one objective.
  2. Scaling COX-2 promotion before outcome-level safety evidence exists.
  3. Counting PBM revenue as growth. The PBM supplied about two-thirds of last year's revenue gain.
- **Most urgent brutal fact:** about $5B of drug sales, roughly one-third of the ~$15.3B drug business, loses U.S. patent protection within a few years. Double-digit growth from that position is arithmetically implausible.
- **Missing numbers that would change this:** COX-2 cardiovascular safety data, the PBM's standalone profit, and the pipeline beyond COX-2.
- **Confidence:** moderate. The verdict rests on the dossier alone.

Greatness potential: 55/100

## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Halden is a research company first and last. Its one test for every decision is whether it produces medicines that physicians, patients and regulators can trust completely. It tells investors the truth about the patent cliff and accepts a few years of slower growth rather than manufacturing growth through pharmacy-benefit revenue, a mega-merger or one hyped drug class. Its COX-2 painkiller goes to market only as fast as outcome-level evidence on both stomach and cardiovascular safety allows. It either separates the PBM or rebuilds it around the research engine. Its best scientists work on the post-cliff pipeline. Its founding rule, medicine for the people with profits following, works again as an operating rule and not as a slogan. Its next generation of leaders, including scientist-leaders, can run it without the current CEO.

## 2. The Hedgehog Concept

**Sentence (working hypothesis):** *Be the world's best at turning fundamental biology into proven breakthrough medicines for widespread chronic disease, and let the profits follow.*

| Circle | Candidates considered | Selected | Explicitly NOT best at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best in the world at | Drug discovery broadly; managed pharmaceutical care; first-in-class medicines for large chronic diseases | **Translating fundamental research into first-in-class or best-in-class medicines for large chronic diseases** (cholesterol, heart, osteoporosis) | Pharmacy-benefit management and distribution; generics; low-cost manufacturing; winning class races by marketing |
| Economic engine | Profit per prescription; revenue per member (PBM); profit per R&D dollar; **profit per breakthrough medicine** | **Profit per patent-protected breakthrough medicine** | Revenue volume, since PBM revenue inflates the top line with unknown margin |
| Passion | Growth; scale; science; the founding ethic | **The science, and the founding ethic that medicine is for the people** | "Top-tier growth" as an end in itself |

**Denominator:** profit per breakthrough medicine.

**Confirmation tests:**
- **Does it explain past wins?** Yes. Cholesterol ($4.7B), heart/blood pressure ($4.2B) and the growing osteoporosis drug (+46%, from $532M to $775M) are all research-born medicines for large chronic diseases.
- **Does it rule things out?** Yes. It rules out PBM expansion, a gap-filling mega-merger, and me-too launches.
- **Would committing to it be uncomfortable?** Yes. It means publicly giving up the double-digit growth promise through the cliff.
- **Confirmed if** drug-business profit per approved breakthrough holds or rises over the next three to five years. **Disconfirmed if** the PBM's standalone economics prove it raises drug-business profit, in which case widen the concept.

## 3. The flywheel

```
   ┌──► 1. Fundamental research on disease biology
   │            │
   │            ▼
   │    2. Breakthrough (first/best-in-class) medicines for large chronic diseases
   │            │
   │            ▼
   │    3. Rigorous outcome-level evidence of benefit AND safety   ◄── WEAKEST LINK
   │            │
   │            ▼
   │    4. Trust from physicians, patients, regulators
   │            │
   │            ▼
   │    5. Profit per patent-protected breakthrough medicine  (denominator)
   │            │
   │            ▼
   └─── 6. Reinvestment in research + attracting the best scientists
```

The PBM does not sit in this loop. Its only plausible connection is safety surveillance (see Gate 7).

**Current push (the next two turns):**
1. **Turn 3:** make the planned COX-2 long-term outcome studies decisive. They should have prespecified cardiovascular safety endpoints and independent monitoring, and their results should gate how far promotion scales.
2. **Turn 6 → 1:** put the strongest scientists on the post-cliff pipeline, and protect the research budget even while profits are under pressure.

## 5. Stop-doing list (stewardship mode: real temptations only)

1. **Stop naming "top-tier growth company" as the number-one business objective.** Put the founding purpose back first.
2. **Stop issuing double-digit growth promises** that the pipeline cannot support.
3. **Refuse to scale COX-2 promotion ahead of outcome-level cardiovascular and stomach safety evidence.** Refuse to let one drug class carry the growth story.
4. **Stop counting PBM revenue growth as company growth** in investor messaging, and freeze PBM expansion capital until standalone economics are known.
5. **Keep refusing a gap-filling mega-merger.** The CEO already says this; hold the line under pressure.
6. **Refuse to cut research spending to protect earnings per share** through the patent cliff.

## 7. Core ideology and the BHAG

- **Core values:**
  - Medicine is for the people; profits follow.
  - Scientific excellence.
  - Honesty in evidence: we publish and act on what the data say.
  - Patient safety over speed.
- **Core purpose:** to discover medicines that make people's lives longer and better.
- **BHAG (20-year horizon):** discover more first-in-class medicines with proven long-term outcome benefit than any other company in the world.
