# Good to Great: Larkspur Financial

## One-screen verdict

**Hedgehog Concept (working hypothesis):** *Be the bank that American households and small businesses choose as their primary financial home, and earn more per actively used relationship than anyone else.* Relationships customers use count. Products merely sold do not.

**Weakest flywheel link:** "Customer adds a product they actually use." The current engine counts products opened, under daily quotas and per-product pay. Nothing in the dossier shows how many of those products are funded or used.

**Top three stop-doing items:**
1. Stop setting daily and monthly product-sales quotas for store team members.
2. Stop paying incentives on product count and cross-sell.
3. Stop using "eight products" and raw cross-sell as the headline goal and the investor metric.

**Most urgent brutal fact:** In July, the central bank found misconduct at the former consumer-finance subsidiary: steering borrowers into worse loans and falsifying incomes. It said the conduct was allegedly fostered by incentive pay and sales quotas. The flagship Community Banking business runs the same kind of system, at much larger scale. Risk and HR report into the business lines that are paid on sales.

**Missing numbers that would change this answer:**
- The share of cross-sold products that are funded and active.
- Sales-practice terminations and ethics-hotline volume.
- Branch team-member turnover.
- How store sales goals are set and escalated.
- Profit per household by *active* product depth.

If funded and active rates are high and misconduct signals are low, most CONDITIONALs move to PASS. If they are low, cross-sell becomes a liability on the balance of trust, and Gate 1 has to be redone.

Greatness potential: 35/100

## 1. The great version in one paragraph

A great Larkspur is the bank that one in three American households, and a large share of small businesses, use to run their financial lives because they chose it, not because someone opened something for them. Its edge is the low-cost, sticky deposit and payments relationship built through the community-banking culture. It grows earnings by deepening relationships that customers actively use, and it can prove that with funded and active data. Store team members are paid for customer outcomes such as retention, active use and satisfaction, not for product counts. A central, independent risk and conduct function can see problems across every business unit and stop them. Decentralization stays wide in how units serve customers, and stays narrow in how they are measured, paid and policed. It outperforms through the cycle on returns without needing the next acquisition or the next quota increase to do it.

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Stop daily and monthly product-sales quotas** for store team members.
2. **Stop per-product and cross-sell-based incentive pay.** Replace it with active-use, retention and customer satisfaction, with clawbacks for accounts closed unused or opened without consent.
3. **Stop presenting "eight products" and raw cross-sell** as the strategic goal and the headline investor metric. Report funded and active relationships alongside it, then instead of it.
4. **Stop letting risk management and HR report into the business lines** they oversee.
5. **Stop chasing mortgage origination share for its own sake.** Being largest originator is volume, not relationship, and it is where this year's consent orders came from.
6. **Stop framing regulatory findings as "industry-wide."** Treat the consent orders, the $85M fine and the redlining suit as one root-cause autopsy.
7. **Stop pursuing large acquisitions to backfill fading integration savings.**
8. **Stop treating decentralization as all-or-nothing.** Autonomy is for serving customers, not for measuring, paying or policing conduct.

**Non-negotiable disciplines:**
- A product counts only if it has documented customer consent and is funded or used within a set window `[ASSUMPTION: 90 days]`.
- Independent risk can veto any sales program or pay plan.
- The board sees conduct red flags every quarter: complaints, hotline reports, terminations, unfunded-account rates and turnover.
- Every regulatory finding gets a blameless enterprise-wide autopsy within 60 days.

**Framework boundaries:**
- **Business units decide:** product design, service model, local market tactics, staffing mix.
- **The center decides:** what is measured, how people are paid, risk appetite, conduct standards.

**Bureaucracy check:** Daily quotas are a control system that substitutes for trusting the right people. With the right people on the bus and good measures of customer outcomes, the quotas should not be needed.

## 7. Core ideology and the BHAG

**Core purpose:** Help American households and small businesses succeed financially.

**Core values (unchanging):**
1. The customer's interest comes before the sale.
2. Integrity over volume: a number we can't defend is a number we don't report.
3. Team members are trusted owners of outcomes, not quota-fillers.
4. Bad news travels fast and safely.
5. Community: be part of the places we serve.

**BHAG (15-year horizon):** One in three American households uses Larkspur as its primary bank *by verified active use*, with the highest customer-trust scores of any large U.S. bank.

**Clock-building mechanisms:**
- Independent CRO with a board reporting line.
- Central pay governance with clawbacks.
- Conduct analytics that flag unfunded, duplicate or anomalous account openings.
- Board-visible ethics hotline with non-retaliation protections.
- A separate chair or lead independent director.
- A documented CEO and business-head succession plan.
