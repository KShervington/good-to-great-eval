# Good to Great: Annika Sorell's career

## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Within a few years, Annika spends most of her scanning time on complex adult congenital studies: Fontan patients, 3D datasets and TEE support. She works in a seat built for that work, at the university's new team or in a congenital/advanced-imaging role at her current hospital. She is paid as a specialist and does fewer, harder studies each day, so her income per study rises while the load on her shoulder falls. The cardiologists who ask for her now become her sponsors. Her Fontan protocol and case library become the regional standard. She teaches congenital scanning to students and colleagues, which deepens her expertise and gives her a source of income that doesn't depend on her scanning arm. If she were gone, the region's adult congenital patients would notice. Her career would also survive a bad shoulder year, because it no longer depends entirely on volume.

## 2. The Hedgehog Concept

| Circle | Candidates | Verdict |

**Denominator: income per study.** This number exposes the treadmill.
- At $46.40/hr on an 8-hour shift `[ASSUMPTION: 8-hr base shift]`, base pay is about $371 a day.
- At 9 studies/day (last year) that is ~$41 per study. At 13/day (now) it is **~$28.50 per study, a drop of about 30%** in one year, for the same work, with more load on an injured shoulder.
- At the same hourly rate, a 75-minute congenital study earns about $58 and a 40-minute routine study about $31. Today that difference comes only from time, not from any premium. The great version adds a real premium.
- Open question for the physio: is a long, complex study easier on the shoulder per hour than back-to-back routine studies? Don't assume it is.

**Tests:**
- *Does it explain past wins?* Yes: the name requests, the Fontan protocol, the self-taught 3D, and the joy she gets from mentoring.
- *Does it rule things out?* Yes: travel, overtime, lab lead, and the generic adjunct lab for now.
- *Is it uncomfortable to commit to?* Yes. It means turning down the fastest money, and it may mean leaving an employer of 11 years or taking on a 70-minute commute.

**Confirmation tests (it stays a hypothesis until these come back):**
1. A real figure from the university (or a specialist pay band at her current hospital) that matches or beats her current total pay.
2. Her daily study count falls while her income holds.
3. There is demand when she offers to teach congenital content.

## 7. Core ideology and the BHAG

- **Core values:** Get it right for the patient. Stay curious about the hard cases. Teach what you know. Protect the instrument (her body).
- **Core purpose:** Make sure adults born with broken hearts get images clear enough for their doctors to see what's really there.
- **BHAG (kept to one line, as this is a personal subject):** *By 50, be the person who trained the region's next generation of congenital echo sonographers.*
- **Clock-building mechanisms:** written protocols (Fontan, then others), a de-identified case library, a trained second sonographer, an income floor (LTD plus savings), and teaching as income that doesn't depend on her scanning arm.
