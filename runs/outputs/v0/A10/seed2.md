## 1. The great version in one paragraph

The great Harwell-Brandt is no longer a conglomerate. It is a focused consumer-health company that applies the rigor of a pharmaceutical house to a narrow set of everyday remedies: oral care (mouthwash, and possibly sugar-free gum), throat (cough drops) and stomach (antacid and effervescent remedies).

Every health claim it makes is backed by clinical evidence it would defend in court. Ideally it has already been tested there. It holds the #1 brand in each category it chooses to compete in, and it uses its 140-country distribution to take those leaders country by country.

It does not own bakeries, optical labs or a #2 razor business. It does not chase "revive pharma" unless a narrow, best-in-world pipeline earns its place. Capital goes only to positions it leads or can credibly lead. Over time it earns returns well above its cost of capital, and the result does not depend on any single executive or acquisition.
