## 1. The great version in one paragraph

The great Kessling does not try to be a smaller copy of the largest research-based drug companies. It is the company that physicians in endocrinology, metabolic disease and rheumatology think of first. It produces more first-in-class hormone and anti-inflammatory medicines per research dollar than anyone else, and it makes them at the lowest cost through its fermentation and steroid process chemistry. Its safety evidence is the most trusted in its field because it finds its own problems before regulators do. In-licensing fills gaps inside its chosen fields and never stands in for its own discovery. Non-drug ventures are gone or fully separated. The board is majority independent, and the chief executive is chosen for fit with the mission, not for family ties. The founding family stays an owner and steward of the values: employee welfare, civic responsibility and scientific integrity.
