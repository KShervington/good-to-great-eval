## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Corwin is a focused household-paper company and nothing else. It has a handful of leading towel and tissue brands. Each brand wins blind tests on what consumers actually feel (softness, strength, absorbency). Each is made in modernized, environmentally compliant mills at the lowest unit cost in the industry, and each is supported by advertising the brand's own margin can pay for. It has sold the businesses it can't be best at. It uses the proceeds to fund mill modernization and product science. It holds or regains first place region by region against Rival P, and it measures itself on profit per brand. It is run by a chairman who credits the organization, takes the blame personally, and develops a successor from inside.

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. Stop acquiring outside household paper, including "growth industry" deals that look attractive.
2. Stop running non-core units as permanent portfolio pieces. Run a sale process for films, fine papers, furniture and teaching aids.
3. Stop describing the core as a mature cash cow to be milked for diversification.
4. Stop governing by committee. One accountable chairman, with clear lines of decision.
5. Stop launching products outside the Hedgehog Concept (for example, a second diaper attempt) until the core flywheel turns again.
6. Stop making pricing doctrine without data. Neither "never coupon" nor "cut list price" stays policy without regional test results.
7. Stop broad modernization. Spend capex only on mills that make core brands, beyond what environmental rules require.

**Non-negotiable disciplines:**
- Every capital or deal decision passes a written Hedgehog Concept test.
- Brand-level profit and loss is reviewed monthly.
- A blind-test review against the leading rival happens every year.

**Freedom within the framework:** brand teams choose their own advertising and promotion tactics. Mills choose their own cost programs. Nobody chooses a new category.

**Bureaucracy check:** the office of the chairman was structure compensating for the lack of a single right leader. Don't rebuild it in another form.

**Gate 6 verdict:** `PASS`.
