## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Stop Homeownership Pathways.** Decline the program and refer interested clients to an existing housing-counseling agency. It's outside the core, it uses restricted money, and the funder has a conflict. Its indirect allowance (about $37–44K a year, depending on how the 18% is applied) doesn't close the gap it would open in management attention.
2. **Stop the statewide campaign and the policy doubling.** The policy unit stays at two staff and works on Marlow.
3. **Stop screening out tenants just because their case looks hard to win.** Screen for where a lawyer changes the outcome. Drop the rental-assistance-match criterion as those funds wind down.
4. **Stop leading reports with the win rate.** Lead with households housed, coverage per 100 filings, and per attorney-year.
5. **Stop letting partners design core programs.** The firm and the bank advise; EHDC decides.
6. **Stop waiting for the new system to measure.** Use an interim spreadsheet with docket numbers now, and retire the weekly paper re-entry as soon as possible.
7. **Stop meeting mobility-limited clients in the lobby.** Book an accessible private room in advance, because the elevator outage keeps recurring.
