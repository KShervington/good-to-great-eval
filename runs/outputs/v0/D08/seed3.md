# Good to Great: Eastbank Housing Defense Collaborative (EHDC)

## Verdict

**Hedgehog Concept (working hypothesis):** *EHDC is Marlow County's eviction-court defense system. It puts the right level of legal help on every tenant who comes through housing court, and keeps more of them housed per filing than anyone else could.*

**Most urgent brutal fact:** EHDC's need grew while its reach shrank, and its headline numbers hide this.
- County eviction filings rose 22% (6,152 to 7,486). Full-representation cases fell 25% (1,688 to 1,274).
- Housed outcomes per 100 county filings fell from **21.7 to 15.0**, even though the success rate rose from 79% to 88%.
- The 93% win rate rose after a Q2 screening change that routes the hardest cases to an advice desk that tracks no outcomes.
- The county contract funds full representation, is 45% of revenue, and comes up for renewal next year.

**Weakest link in the flywheel:** proof. EHDC cannot show the county or its funders how many filed tenants it keeps housed, because desk outcomes are on paper or not recorded at all.

**Top three stop-doing items:**
1. Decline or defer Homeownership Pathways ($735K). The treasurer should be recused from the vote.
2. Stop leading with the win rate. Report outcomes per county filing instead.
3. Stop letting "where we can win" set intake priority. Triage by need and leverage, and measure every tier.

**Missing numbers that would change this answer:**
- Advice-desk outcomes.
- Outcome rates before and after the Q2 protocol change.
- County contract terms (fixed or per-case, any minimum case counts).
- Cost per program.
- The wind-down schedule for rental assistance.

Greatness potential: 42/100

*Evidence note: this run had no memory, past-chat search or web research. The dossier (the ED's funder report plus the program officer's site-visit notes) is the only evidence. Every inference is marked `[ASSUMPTION]`.*

## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great EHDC is the backbone of Marlow County's eviction docket. It is not a boutique that wins the cases it chooses.
- Every tenant with a filing is offered a tier of help: full representation, limited-scope attorney help, or a supervised desk session.
- Every tier is measured against the same public court record.
- The organization can say each month how many of the county's filed households stayed housed or moved on their own timeline, and what EHDC contributed.
- That number is what the county renews, what funders buy, and what the attorneys take pride in.
- It turns down attractive work outside the courthouse, such as homeownership counseling and statewide campaigns.
- It uses its policy capacity only where Marlow County court practice changes outcomes. That includes, over time, building toward a county right to counsel with EHDC as its delivery engine.
- It runs on a balanced budget with reserves that let it ride out a contract cycle.
- It will outlast its founder.

## 2. The Hedgehog Concept

**Sentence:** *Be Marlow County's eviction-court defense system: the right level of legal help on every tenant in housing court, and more households kept housed per filing than anyone else could manage.*

**Denominator (resource engine):** **housed outcomes per 100 county eviction filings.**
- A housed outcome means the tenant is still housed, or moved on their own timeline with 60+ days.
- The count covers all service tiers, verified against court dockets.
- This replaces "win rate among cases we chose," which can be improved simply by choosing better cases.

**Intersection tests:**
- **Explains past wins?** Yes. County satisfaction, fast hearings and the rental-assistance results all come from courthouse defense.
- **Rules things out?** Yes. It excludes Homeownership, statewide expansion, and treating the win rate as the goal.
- **Uncomfortable?** Yes. It makes this year's numbers look worse, turns down $735K, and tells the pro bono partners that their intake logic was only half right.

**Confirmation tests (hypothesis, not proven):**
1. A docket-matched sample of advice-desk contacts shows a measurable housed-outcome rate, so the desk is real defense and not just referral.
2. The county confirms that coverage and outcomes per filing matter in the renewal.
3. Housed outcomes per 100 filings rise for four consecutive quarters as filings climb.

**Alternatives rejected:**
- "Best win rate in housing defense" optimizes selection.
- "A housing ladder from eviction to ownership" stretches beyond what EHDC can be best at.

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Stop Homeownership Pathways.** Decline it, or defer it at least until the county renewal is secured.
   - The money: $245K a year for three years. The 18% indirect works out to about $37–44K a year, depending on how it is calculated.
   - That indirect would cover most of the $63K deficit on paper, which is exactly what makes it dangerous. It is restricted money for work EHDC cannot be best at, with a cliff in year 3.
   - The demand signal is weak: 41% of 412 survey respondents said "someday."
   - Instead, offer a no-cost referral pathway to HUD-approved counselors.
2. **Stop leading with the win rate** in funder reports, board decks and staff celebrations. Lead with housed outcomes per 100 filings, broken out by tier.
3. **Stop setting intake priority by "where we can win."** Triage is right; selection for win rate is not. Every screened-out tenant gets a defined tier, and that tier is measured.
4. **Stop logging desk contacts on paper while waiting for spring.** Start a structured digital form this month, even a spreadsheet, with fields for case number and outcome.
5. **Do not double the policy unit or launch a statewide campaign.** Revisit only if the per-filing number rises and the county contract is renewed.
6. **Stop plugging deficits from reserves or with out-of-scope restricted grants.** Set a balanced-budget rule and a reserve floor.
7. **Stop meeting mobility-limited clients in the office lobby.** Book an accessible private room, or meet at the courthouse, until the elevator is reliable.

## 7. Core ideology and BHAG

**Core purpose:** No Marlow County household loses its home without a fair fight.

**Core values:**
1. Every tenant in housing court gets help, at the right tier.
2. Tell the truth with numbers, including the unflattering ones.
3. The client's timeline and dignity come first.
4. Win in the courtroom and in the record. Rigor is respect.

**BHAG (15-year horizon):** *Every tenant facing eviction in Marlow County has a lawyer or a lawyer-supervised advocate, and most of them stay housed.*
- This is a county right to counsel, delivered by EHDC.
- It is also the only policy goal that sits inside the Hedgehog Concept.

**Mechanisms (clock building):**
- A monthly per-filing dashboard, reviewed by the board.
- A written triage framework, owned by the managing attorney, not by any partner.
- A one-page Hedgehog test that every new program must pass before a board vote.
- A conflict-of-interest recusal policy.
- A reserve-floor policy.
- A documented ED succession plan. The founder is in year 11.
