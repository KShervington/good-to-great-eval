## 1. The great version in one paragraph

A great EHDC is the reason eviction in Marlow County is a fair fight. Every tenant who receives a filing is identified from clerk data and matched to the right help before the first hearing: full representation where an attorney changes the outcome most, the advice desk where guided self-help is enough, and rental assistance where it exists. EHDC measures itself on one number the county, funders and staff all understand: **households kept housed per 100 eviction filings**. It reports that number, along with cost per household housed, every quarter, including the years when it falls. It turns down attractive money outside eviction defense. Its two-person policy unit uses its own data to change local court practice and to make the case for county-wide coverage. It runs on written mechanisms, not on its founder. That matters because the founder has led for eleven years and should eventually be able to leave without the organization slipping.

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Stop headlining the win rate** in reports, the annual dinner and funder materials unless coverage per 100 filings appears next to it.
2. **Stop the Homeownership Pathways proposal** as designed. Refer interested clients to an existing housing counselor and run no program.
3. **Stop the statewide-campaign expansion.** Do not double the policy unit for state work.
4. **Stop screening full representation on "can we win" alone.** Screen on *how much an attorney changes the outcome*. A tenant with a strong defense may do fine with desk help, while a tenant with no defense may need an attorney to get 60+ days and a sealed record `[ASSUMPTION: hypothesis to test with outcome data]`.
5. **Stop building the intake screen on rental-assistance matches.** The state program is winding down, and that criterion is losing its value.
6. **Stop letting sponsors or funders design programs.** This covers both the intake design shaped by pro bono partners and the board treasurer driving a program funded by his own bank.
7. **Stop running deficits for new things.** No restricted grant for work outside the Hedgehog should be justified by its indirect-cost allowance.
8. **Stop meeting clients in the lobby** as the default when the elevator fails. Arrange an accessible, private alternative.
