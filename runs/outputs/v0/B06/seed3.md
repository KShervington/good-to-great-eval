# Halden Laboratories: Good to Great run (as of January, Year 0)

## Verdict

**Hedgehog Concept (working hypothesis):** *Halden discovers first-in-class medicines and proves, more rigorously than anyone, that they help patients, so that physicians trust each one and every protected year of it earns a premium.*

**Weakest flywheel link:** **Rigorous proof → physician trust.** The growth objective is pushing the COX-2 painkiller into the investor story before its long-term outcome and safety data exist.

**Top 3 stop-doing:**
1. Stop naming "top-tier growth company" as the #1 objective. Halden's research engine cannot meet it through the patent cliff.
2. Stop counting PBM revenue as research-company growth. In Year −1 the PBM supplied about two-thirds of headline sales growth.
3. Stop selling the COX-2 drug as the growth story before its outcome studies, with pre-specified cardiovascular endpoints, are designed and reporting.

**Most urgent brutal fact:** The double-digit promise depends heavily on one drug class whose long-term safety is still unknown. Halden's incentive to overlook risk is strongest exactly where its evidence is thinnest.

**Missing numbers that would change this answer:**
- COX-2 cardiovascular-safety data, the biggest swing factor.
- Standalone PBM profitability and return on capital.
- Pipeline beyond the COX-2 compounds.
- R&D spend and productivity.
- Forward earnings guidance.

Greatness potential: 40/100

## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Halden is a research company again, not a growth company that does research. It accepts a few years of slower, honestly reported growth through the patent valley instead of disguising the gap with PBM pass-through revenue or one heavily promoted drug class. Its distinctive asset is trust: when Halden says a medicine works and is safe, physicians, regulators and payers believe it, because Halden runs the hardest outcome studies first and publishes bad news fast. That trust earns premium uptake for each breakthrough. Premium uptake funds the next wave of fundamental research. The founding line that profits follow medicine becomes an operating rule rather than a slogan.

## 2. The Hedgehog Concept

| Circle | Candidates considered | Narrowed to |
|---|---|---|
| **Best in the world at** | Breadth of patents; cardiovascular franchise; managed pharmaceutical care (PBM); mechanism-based discovery; clinical proof | **Mechanism-based discovery of first-in-class drugs plus rigorous outcome proof.** Evidence: on track to patent more new compounds than any rival; decades of reputation for science; built the leading cholesterol franchise. It can **not** be best at PBM operations, generics, or price-based distribution. |
| **Economic engine** | Profit per sale; profit per customer life (PBM); profit per R&D dollar; profit per breakthrough | **Profit per proven breakthrough medicine**, i.e. per patent-protected, clinically differentiated molecule. |
| **Deep passion** | Growth ranking; scale; science that helps patients | **Science that measurably helps patients** ("medicine is for the people"). The growth ranking is the CEO's objective, not the passion of the people `[ASSUMPTION]`. |

**Denominator:** profit per proven breakthrough medicine.

**Tests:**
- *Explains past wins?* Yes. The cholesterol and blood-pressure franchises are first-in-class or best-in-class medicines with strong evidence.
- *Rules things out?* Yes. It excludes the PBM as a growth engine, me-too follow-ons, and a mega-merger.
- *Uncomfortable?* Yes. It means abandoning the public double-digit growth promise.

**Confirmation evidence (12–24 months):**
- COX-2 outcome studies show a net-benefit safety profile.
- Pharma-only profit holds up better than consensus through the cliff.
- At least two non-COX-2 candidates enter late-stage development with differentiated mechanisms.

## 3. The flywheel

```
   ┌─► Fundamental research on new mechanisms
   │              │

   │   First-in-class molecules (most patents in industry)
   │              │
   │              ▼
   │   RIGOROUS PROOF: outcome studies, pre-specified safety   ◄── weakest link
   │              │
   │              ▼
   │   Physician / regulator / payer trust → fast, premium uptake
   │              │
   │              ▼
   │   Profit per proven breakthrough (denominator)
   │              │
   │              ▼
   └── Reinvest in research + attract best scientists
```

**Current push (next two turns):**
1. Make the COX-2 outcome programme the proof standard. Use independent cardiovascular-event adjudication, pre-registered endpoints and comparator choices that answer the safety question rather than flatter the drug. Halden's own cardiovascular expertise and patient base make it the company best placed to do this well.
2. Separate pharma reporting from PBM reporting, so the flywheel's real output is visible to investors and to management.

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. Stop stating "top-tier growth company" as the #1 objective.
2. Stop guiding to or implying double-digit consolidated growth through the patent cliff.
3. Stop reporting PBM revenue blended with pharma revenue as one growth story.
4. Stop promoting the COX-2 drug to investors or physicians beyond what completed data show. Make no broad-population claims until outcome studies report.
5. Stop letting launch timelines shape safety-study design (endpoints, comparators, duration).
6. Stop chasing follow-on or me-too compounds that are not first-in-class, including a second COX-2 unless it is differentiated.
7. Stop any use of the PBM formulary that favors Halden products. This is a trust and conflict risk `[ASSUMPTION: current practice unknown]`.
8. Keep refusing the mega-merger. Make it explicit policy.

## 7. Core ideology and BHAG

- **Core purpose:** to preserve and improve human life through medicines that measurably help patients.
- **Core values:** patients before profit (profits follow); scientific integrity; candor about risk; excellence in discovery; trust earned by evidence.
- **BHAG (20-year):** By Year +20, Halden's word on a medicine's benefit and safety should be the most trusted in the world, proven by at least ten first-in-class medicines with demonstrated outcome benefit.
- **Clock-building mechanisms:**
  - An independent safety officer with a board line and authority over promotional claims.
  - Pre-registered outcome-trial standards.
  - Executive pay tied to multi-year pharma-only metrics, not consolidated EPS growth.
  - A scientist succession bench.
