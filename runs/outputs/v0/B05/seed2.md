# Good to Great: Varden Communications

## Verdict

**Hedgehog Concept (working hypothesis):** Varden should be the best in the world at engineering the radio link between people. That means being first to make each new generation of wireless reliable, small and affordable at Six Sigma quality, even when that makes its own products obsolete. Its economic engine should be measured as **profit per wireless subscriber connected**.

**Weakest flywheel link:** the jump to the next radio standard. The company's own passion is renewal, yet its cellular business is defending analog at the moment carriers are planning digital. The second weak link is carrier trust, which the premium distribution program puts at risk.

**Top three stop-doing items:**
1. Stop running the company on "double in size every five years." Run it on profit per subscriber and digital readiness.
2. Stop the coercive premium distribution program for the new phone. Carriers are customers for both phones and infrastructure.
3. Stop adding unconditional exposure to the satellite venture. Make no full-launch-phase commitment without independent demand evidence and capped subcontract liability.

**Most urgent brutal fact:** Varden's ~45% world handset share sits mostly on analog phones. Carriers are starting to plan digital networks, competing digital standards are emerging, and nobody in the dossier can say what Varden's digital share is. A senior cellular leader says tens of millions of analog customers "cannot be wrong." Success is talking, not data.

**Numbers that would change this answer:**
- **Digital handset share by standard.** If Varden already leads digital, the most urgent fact softens a lot.
- **Profit by segment.** This confirms or kills the denominator and the microprocessor verdict.
- **The satellite venture's demand forecasts.** These decide whether the $6.5B contract exposure is a bet or a hazard.
- **Carrier reaction to the distribution program.**

Greatness potential: 30/100

Everything below rests on the dossier alone, and inferences are marked `[ASSUMPTION]`. More than half the gates (6 of 11) came out CONDITIONAL. ## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Varden is narrower than today's Varden. It is a wireless company first and only. It treats each new radio standard as the moment to obsolete its own best-selling products before a competitor does. It ships digital handsets and infrastructure across the standards carriers actually choose, at Six Sigma quality. It wins carriers because its equipment is the most reliable, not because contracts force shelf space. Its semiconductors exist mainly to make its radios better, smaller and cheaper. It measures itself by profit per subscriber connected, not by sales doubling. Long-range bets like the satellite system are still made, but they are staged, capped and killable. They are never open-ended obligations that could sink the core.

## 2. The Hedgehog Concept

**Sentence:** *Be first and best in the world at engineering each new generation of the radio link between people, at Six Sigma quality, and win on profit per subscriber connected.*

| Circle | Candidates considered | Kept | Ruled out (cannot be best in world) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Best in world at** | Wireless equipment; manufacturing quality; semiconductors; consumer devices; global systems | Radio-link engineering across handsets, infrastructure and two-way radio (No. 1 in phones, paging and two-way radio); Six Sigma quality at scale | General-purpose PC microprocessors (Varden is the challenger to a dominant supplier, via an alliance); operating a satellite phone service; computing and data products; consumer retail marketing `[ASSUMPTION]` |
| **Economic engine** | Profit per unit sold; per carrier; per subscriber; per patent | **Profit per wireless subscriber connected** (handset plus infrastructure plus service-enabling equipment, divided by subscribers on Varden-equipped networks) `[ASSUMPTION]` | Sales volume. Revenue grew 21.6% last year while earnings grew 14.1%, so size is not the engine. |
| **Passion** | Quality; technical debate; long-range bets; self-renewal | Self-obsolescence and engineering renewal; quality | Defending the installed base (this is pride, not passion) |

**Tests**
- **Does it explain past wins?** Yes. It covers the car radio to two-way radio to cellular arc, the exits from TV and car radio, and the quality award.
- **Does it rule things out?** Yes. It excludes the PC chip war, computing products, satellite service operation, and coercive distribution.
- **Is it uncomfortable to commit to?** Yes. It requires obsoleting the analog franchise and the proud new analog phone.

**Confirmation evidence:**
- Segment profit shows wireless profit per subscriber is stable or rising, even under handset price pressure.
- Digital products win carrier commitments in at least the leading standards within 12 months.
- Semiconductors earn more when aligned to communications than to PCs.

## 3. The flywheel

```
   ┌─► 1. Lead the next radio standard early
   │        (renewal: obsolete our own products first)
   │                     │
   │                     ▼
   │   2. Six Sigma quality at scale ──► lower cost, higher reliability
   │                     │
   │                     ▼
   │   3. Carriers choose Varden on merit (handsets + infrastructure + radio)
   │                     │
   │                     ▼
   │   4. More subscribers on Varden equipment
   │        ──► PROFIT PER SUBSCRIBER CONNECTED
   │                     │
   │                     ▼
   └── 5. Fund R&D and standards work for the next generation
```

**Current push:**
- Push link 1: commit to digital across the emerging standards now, and make the analog clamshell the last flagship analog product.
- Push link 3: rebuild carrier trust by dropping or redesigning the distribution program.

**Doom-loop check (signs present):**
- **Size-driven targets.** "Double every five years" means about $54B by around Year 4, which needs ~15% compound growth. It invites growth for its own sake.
- **Big-bet jump-starts.** The satellite exposure is about $560M in equity and commitments plus $6.5B in contracts. The contracts alone are about 3.7× last year's net earnings.
- **Leverage instead of merit.** The distribution program substitutes contract terms for earning carrier loyalty.
- **Scattered fronts.** Microprocessors, wireless data, China and satellite are all being pursued at once.

None of these is fatal yet. Together they show a Stage 1→2 drift (see section 11).

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. Stop using "double every five years" as the governing goal.
2. Stop the premium distribution program as designed. At minimum, pause it until carriers are interviewed.
3. Stop launching new flagship analog-only phones after the current clamshell.
4. Stop adding unconditional satellite subcontract obligations. Make no full-launch commitment without independent demand data, a capped downside, and a kill criterion.
5. Stop fighting the dominant PC chip supplier for general-purpose PCs. Redirect that silicon toward communications and embedded uses `[ASSUMPTION: feasible without breaking alliance obligations]`.
6. Stop investing in computing and data products that don't feed the radio link. Review them for divestiture.
7. Stop treating emerging-market investment as a volume play. Fund it only where it builds subscribers on Varden networks.

**Non-negotiable disciplines:**
- Six Sigma on every new product.
- A digital-readiness review before any handset program is funded.
- Every major bet is staged, with a written kill condition.

**Framework and freedom:** inside wireless, divisions have full autonomy over products and engineering. Outside wireless, nothing is funded without board-level Hedgehog justification.

**Bureaucracy check:** the distribution program's contract terms are a control mechanism that stands in for winning carriers on product merit.

**Charisma check.** The authority of a successful franchise leader, and executive pride in the new phone, are suppressing the digital signal.

**Faith statement:** *Varden has renewed itself through every radio transition for 70 years. It will win digital the same way, by being the first to make its own analog lead obsolete.*

## 7. Core ideology and BHAG

**Core values:**
1. Renewal: obsolete ourselves before anyone else can.
2. Quality without compromise: Six Sigma.
3. Open technical debate, all the way to the top.
4. Integrity with customers: win on merit.
5. Patient long-range bets, staged and disciplined.

**Core purpose:** to connect people by radio, reliably, wherever they are.

**BHAG (20-year horizon):** half the people on Earth communicate through a radio link Varden engineered.

**Clock-building mechanisms:**
- A standing standards-transition council that decides which generation comes next.
- Stage-gate rules for big bets.
- Carrier-satisfaction metrics in executive pay.
- Six Sigma institutionalized beyond its founders.
- A succession bench that is not limited to the founding family.
