## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Ten years on, when anyone in the region wants to know what road salt does to streams, they come to Elena. The state transportation agency writes its salting rules using her multi-winter, multi-stream record. Her students publish the mechanistic papers that explain why salt pulses collapse invertebrate communities and slow leaf breakdown. Her lab runs on its own federal and agency funding. She still works with the Northern Streams Network, but as a peer who brings her own salt question to it, not as its field manager. The record keeps running without her intervention: written protocols, trained students, an agency-backed monitoring line, and a public archive mean it would outlast any one grant, student or person.
