## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Stop using asset growth and "world's largest" as goals.** Remove them from targets, compensation and public messaging.
2. **Stop new lending growth to developing countries.** Honor existing commitments, set country limits as a percentage of capital, and allow no growth until those limits exist.
3. **Stop originating long-term fixed-rate mortgages for the bank's own book** while they are funded by short-term deposits. Originate adjustable-rate loans, or sell the fixed-rate ones.
4. **Stop decentralized approval of large or concentrated credits.** Local authority stays below a threshold. Anything above it goes to central credit.
5. **Stop making new venture-capital direct investments.**
6. **Stop opening international branches** that don't serve home-state customers' trade. Review the 90+ existing ones against that test.
7. **Stop growing construction and agricultural books faster than deposits and capital.** Farms stay inside the mission, but under concentration caps.
8. **Stop automatically backfilling attrition.** Every replacement has to justify itself against profit per relationship.
9. **Stop applying "action beats indecision" to credit.** In lending, a deliberate "no" is a decision.
