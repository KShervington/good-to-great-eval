## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Tallypaw is the system independent vet clinics use to decide what to stock, when to reorder, and from whom. It works with any distributor and is trusted precisely because it doesn't sell the drugs. Each clinic can see the money it saves on expired stock and the stockouts it avoids, and that proof spreads from practice manager to practice manager. Clinics that adopt inventory stay roughly twice as long, so gross profit per clinic-year rises every year. The company grows mainly through direct and word-of-mouth channels. Any distributor relationship is non-exclusive, and none is a single point of failure. Maya leads the company instead of carrying its sales calendar, and the culture of blameless post-mortems that Jonah models has become how the whole company works. Tallypaw doesn't make dental software and doesn't chase corporate chains. That focus is the reason it is distinctive and not just one more practice-software vendor.
