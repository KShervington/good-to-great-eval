## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Corvane is the dish-machine company that every independent chef in its metros recommends to every other chef. It is not big. It is dense. Each technician covers 40–50+ machines inside a tight zone. Response inside 4 hours runs at 95%+ in every metro. Every machine logs its wash and rinse temperatures, so an unannounced health inspection holds no fear. Machines come from three or four refurbishment channels that Corvane owns relationships with, not from one lucky liquidator. A metro opens only when the last one has hit the density and response standard, and a written playbook gates it, not the founder's appetite. Every machine and every tech makes money after labor. The company could lose Dev, Delmar, or any single customer and still be the fastest, most trusted option in its zones.
