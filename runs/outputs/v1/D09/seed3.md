# Larder Line: Good to Great

## Verdict

**Larder Line is good but not yet great. Its biggest threat is not on the retreat agenda.**

**Hedgehog (working):** *Larder Line is Corbridge's same-day cold chain. It rescues the perishable food others can't move safely and gets it to agencies that will use it that day.*

**Weakest flywheel link:** proof. Pounds are counted at pickup. Nobody knows how much gets eaten or how many households are reached.

**Stop doing:**
1. Running the café as a Larder Line program.
2. Buying a third van now.
3. Composting, backpacks and gleaning.

**Most urgent brutal fact:** at the Eastside Freshway stores, pounds per pickup fell 36% after the markdown app arrived. The app goes chainwide next year, and Freshway supplies 44% of all pounds.

**Numbers that would change this:**
- Eastside pounds broken down by pickup time and by food category.
- Freshway's rollout date.
- How much delivered food agencies use versus compost.

Greatness potential: 35/100

## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Larder Line is the cold chain behind Corbridge's emergency food system. Donors trust it with their highest-value perishables because its vans are temperature-logged and its drivers are trained. These donors include grocers, and increasingly the distributors and wholesalers upstream of them, where markdown apps don't reach. It sends each load the same day to an agency that has asked for it and will use it, so more dairy and protein go out and less bread arrives unwanted. It reports what matters: pounds used and households reached, not only pounds lifted. Its 270 volunteers and long-tenured drivers run a system that would work without its founder. It says no, warmly and often, to good ideas that belong to someone else.

## 2. The Hedgehog Concept

**Sentence:** Larder Line is Corbridge's same-day cold chain. It rescues the perishable food others can't move safely and gets it to agencies that will use it that day.

| **Resource engine** (time, money, brand) | *Time:* drivers stay an average of 3.4 years. *Money:* 64% of revenue is institutional (foundations plus county), and those funders now ask for outcomes. *Brand:* donor trust in its cold-chain handling. | Growth paid for by activity metrics that funders will stop rewarding |
| **Deeply passionate about** | Getting good food to people reliably ("they text before they come"), and logistics craft (Teddy's eleven years in grocery distribution, Kofi's routes). | The café, which is Renata's real passion but not the organization's |

**Denominator:** resources spent and raised **per pound of perishable food actually used by recipients**. It replaces "per pound picked up," which counts bread nobody asked for and produce that gets composted.

**Status:** this is a working hypothesis. The confirmation tests are listed in the appendix under Gate 5.

## 7. Core ideology and the BHAG

- **Core purpose:** no good food wasted while a neighbor goes without.
- **Core values:**
  1. Food safety without compromise.
  2. Show up when we said we would.
  3. Dignity for the people who eat the food.
  4. Waste nothing: not food, not donor dollars, not volunteer hours.
  5. Volunteers are owners.
- **BHAG (about 15 years):** every pound of rescuable dairy, meat and fresh produce in Corbridge reaches a household that needs it on the day it's rescued.

### Gate 6: Disciplines and framework
- **Non-negotiables:**
  - Every reefer route is a cold-chain route.
  - Temperature is logged on every run.
  - Drivers text agencies before arriving.
  - No delivery goes to an agency that hasn't asked for that food.
  - The donor yield review happens monthly.
- **Framework:** Kofi and the drivers decide routing and matching freely, within the cold-chain-first rule. Teddy pursues any donor inside the Hedgehog. Anything outside the Hedgehog goes to a board vote.
- **Hedgehog filter for new requests:** Is it perishable? Is it cold-chain? Is it same-day? Will an agency use it? If not, refer it out.
- **Bureaucracy check:** no heavy compensating rules were found. The risk is adding no-show rules instead of building a reliable Saturday core.
