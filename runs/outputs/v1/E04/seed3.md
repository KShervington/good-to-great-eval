## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Running a commercial airline.** Stop the uncommitted portion of the 15-city, 11-jet expansion, then sell or spin off the airline and aviation services. Its current profitability is the reason to sell now, not the reason to keep it.
2. **Making newsprint and printing paper.** Stop reinvesting in commodity mills and begin a sale or spin-off.
3. **Making tobacco and specialty technical papers.** It is attractive, but it is not the hedgehog.
4. **Building European plants ahead of evidence.** No new capacity without a passed share and return milestone.
5. **Fighting Rival P outside absorbent personal care.** Don't follow it into adjacent packaged-goods categories.
6. **Funding high-price, narrow-use novelties.** The anti-viral tissue is the warning. Innovation must expand a category, not decorate it.
7. **Running any business that cannot report profit per brand.** If it can't be measured on the denominator, it doesn't get capital.
