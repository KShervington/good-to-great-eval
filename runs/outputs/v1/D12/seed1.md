# Bellmoor 250: Good to Great review

## Verdict

**Gate 0: FAIL. The run stops here.** Bellmoor 250 is a ten-day event run by a commission that dissolves by charter, with every staff contract ending alongside it. The framework only works on endeavors that compound over years. Here there is no Hedgehog Concept to find, no flywheel that turns more than once, and no "lasting endurance" this steward can deliver.

**Most urgent brutal fact:** Calder Mills' $160,000 April installment (11.7% of the budget) is at risk. The executive who secured it has retired with no successor named. The parent company has Bellmoor operations "under review." The town's cash-flow loan runs out in May.

**What would change the answer:** the council chartering a permanent successor body (for example, "Bellmoor Days") with its own steward, funding and a multi-year horizon. The framework could then be run on that body.

Greatness potential: 3/100

---

## Why the run stops at Gate 0

The chair asked for the commission to be treated "like any other organization." The honest version of that request is to apply the framework's own fit test, and Bellmoor 250 does not pass it. This is not a judgment on its quality. The plan looks solid, and parts of it (the symphony, the oral history) are in good shape. The framework simply needs time to compound, and this subject is designed to end.

### Fit checklist

| Test | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Ongoing endeavor | **No** | A single ten-day event. The charter requires dissolution. |
| Has a steward | Yes, time-limited | The commission and executive director exist only until dissolution. |
| People and resources can be rearranged | Yes | $1.36M budget, nine subcommittees, 410 volunteers. |
| Time horizon of years | **No** | The commission ends by design. Nothing can compound. |
| Outcomes measurable | Partly | Visits and impressions can be counted, but the targets have no stated basis. "A lasting legacy" is undefined. |

Two of the five tests fail outright, and they are the two the framework depends on. Running the remaining gates anyway would produce artifacts that look like Collins but mislead:

- A flywheel that turns once is just a project plan.
- A Hedgehog Concept takes years of disciplined iteration to find, and this subject has months.
- The Level 5 succession test and *Built to Last* clock-building both assume that something continues after the commission, and nothing does.

## Recommended method instead: a pre-opening risk and decision review

What Bellmoor 250 needs is a time-bound project discipline: a critical-path cash-flow review, a pre-mortem, and explicit decision rules. Two of Collins's habits do transfer to a finite project:

- **Confront the hard facts while keeping faith in the outcome.**
- **Keep a stop-doing list.**

The register below uses them in that limited sense. It is not a substitute for Gates 1–10.

### Risk and decision register (from the dossier)

| # | Issue | Evidence | Severity | Suggested owner | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Calder Mills $160k at risk** | $270k pledge is 62.6% of all sponsorship and 19.8% of the budget. $110k received, $160k due in April. Ed Farrow has retired and no successor is named. The parent is consolidating production elsewhere and Bellmoor operations are "under review." | **Critical** | Chair + ED | Meet Calder leadership now and seek written reconfirmation or an earlier payment. Ask the town to extend the cash-flow loan past May. Build a "minus $160k" budget scenario that names which program elements shrink. |
| 2 | **Payments run ahead of the cash that funds them** | Tall-ship final deposit is due by March, before the April installment. The loan covers deposits only until May. | High | Treasurer | Map month-by-month cash against commitments. Identify the last date each commitment can be reduced or cancelled. |
| 3 | **180,000-visit target has no basis** | It came from a workshop. That is 5.7 visits per resident (population 31,400). The only comparison is a neighboring city's "over 100,000," with no published method. | High | Program chair + ED | Re-estimate bottom-up, event by event, as a low/expected/high range. The target drives the ticket ($121.5k) and merchandise ($70k) projections, safety and transport planning, and possibly sponsor promises. Check whether any sponsor agreement cites it. |
| 4 | **25M "regional media impressions" is undefined** | A major announcement (the tall ship) produced 1.4M. | Medium | Communications volunteer | Define how impressions are counted, or replace the target with outcomes the commission can verify. |
| 5 | **The $96k app is unbudgeted** | $20k is offered, leaving $76k unfunded. The 72% figure is stated intent from 640 students, not usage. Supporters call it the most visible legacy piece, but the commission dissolves and no one owns hosting, app-store accounts or updates afterward. | High (budget) | Steering committee | Stop-doing candidate. Don't commit commission funds while item 1 is unresolved. If pursued, make it conditional on full outside funding by January **and** a named long-term owner, such as the library (which already holds the archive). Alternative: a schedule and ticketing page on an existing platform. |
| 6 | Volunteer shortfall | 410 of 600 signed up (68%). | Medium | Volunteers chair | Set monthly recruitment milestones and a minimum viable staffing plan. |
| 7 | Oral history behind plan | 141 of 240 interviews recorded (59%). Permanent host secured. | Low–Medium | Legacy chair | Protect this. It is the most durable legacy asset the commission has. |
| 8 | **"Lasting legacy" is undefined, and no handoff exists** | The commission dissolves. Assets include the archive, symphony score, time capsule, volunteer list and sponsor relationships. | Medium | Chair + Legacy chair | Write a dissolution handoff plan that names who owns each asset afterward, covering score rights and performance, time-capsule custody and opening date, and volunteer data (with consent). |
| 9 | "Bellmoor Days" proposal | The commission cannot continue by charter. | Outside the commission's remit | Town council | If the council wants it, it should charter a separate body early, so item 8 can hand assets to it. The commission should not take on this work or its costs. |

**Stop-doing (project sense):**

1. Don't approve unbudgeted additions, the app included, until Calder is resolved.
2. Don't treat the 180,000 and 25M figures as commitments until they have a method behind them.
3. Don't spend commission time designing an annual festival the charter forbids.
