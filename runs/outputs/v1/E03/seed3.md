## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Marbury is already mostly visible. It is a self-reliant, science-led company that holds #1 or #2 positions in categories where its products lower the total cost of care. It sells diagnostics that make treatment decisions faster and cheaper, nutrition that prevents costlier care, hospital products that are dependable and efficient, and drugs in niches it can lead. Cost containment works in its favor, because it sells exactly what cost-pressed buyers want. Earnings grow from volume and mix rather than price. R&D is funded from cash, not debt, and goes where leadership is winnable. Its quality is so reliable that regulators and hospitals never have to think about it. The great version does **not** look like a smaller copy of the largest drugmakers, and it does not own a PBM.
