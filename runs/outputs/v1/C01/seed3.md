## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Wexley is a decentralized, budget-disciplined medical-device company. It owns a few niches where it is #1 or #2, led by powered orthopedic instruments. It keeps beds and stretchers as a profitable base, and they do not set its direction. Specialist reps, paid on results, spend their days with surgeons and OR staff. They carry clinical problems back to an engineering team that applies the founder's rule as company law: every product must make caregivers more efficient or lower the cost of care. Every division has a budget, goals and an owner. Earnings grow steadily every year, and the company no longer depends on any one inventor or family member. Rival H's push into implants increases the number of procedures that need Wexley's saws; Wexley does not try to copy it.
