## 5. Stop-doing list

2. **Stop self-insuring your arm.** The LTD opt-out saves about $993 a year (at $38.20 per biweekly paycheck `[ASSUMPTION: biweekly]`), against a risk to roughly $99k a year of income.
3. **Don't take the high-volume travel contract.** Revisit only with university numbers in hand, physio clearance, and a congenital or lower-volume contract.
4. **Don't apply for lab lead, and stop being ambivalent about it.** "Probably not" wastes the leverage. Say no clearly and make your counter-offer.
5. **Stop fitting 75-minute congenital studies into 40-minute routine slots.** Ask for blocked scheduling.
6. **Stop treating the support cushion as the fix.** It patches a load problem. Get a proper ergonomic assessment.
7. **Stop paying for your own specialist training quietly.** The next TEE or 3D training should be funded by your employer or by the employer you're moving to.
