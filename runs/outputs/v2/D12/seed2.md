# Good to Great: Bellmoor 250

## Verdict

**Gate 0 FAIL. Bellmoor 250 is a one-off event run by a body that is chartered to dissolve. The Good to Great framework can't make it "great," so the run stops here.**

- **Hedgehog Concept:** none written. A Hedgehog Concept compounds over years, and this commission ends about 90 days after its final audit.
- **Weakest flywheel link:** there is no flywheel. Nothing feeds back into the next turn.
- **Stop or decide now** (project triage, not a Hedgehog Concept stop-doing list):
  1. Stop planning against 180,000 visits.
  2. Stop deferring the $96,000 app.
  3. Stop committing the March tall-ship deposit without a fallback for Calder's $160,000.
- **Most urgent brutal fact:** one sponsor carries 20% of the budget. Its champion just retired, its parent company has its Bellmoor operations "under review," and its $160,000 installment lands one month before the cash-flow loan cover ends.
- **Missing numbers that would change this:** Calder's written confirmation, an attendance method, and whether the council intends to create a successor body.
- **Confidence:** high on the fit call, because it rests on the charter's dissolution clause. Medium on the risks, because they come from minutes only.

Greatness potential: 4/100

The skill counts a reusable endeavor as ongoing only when it is used repeatedly and someone maintains it. Bellmoor 250 is neither. More importantly, the third test of great is **lasting endurance beyond one person or one lucky break**, and the charter rules that out by design. The framework's core machinery doesn't apply: a flywheel that turns once is just a launch, and Collins describes one big launch as the opposite of how greatness is built.

This says nothing bad about the commission. A commemoration that is excellent and then ends cleanly is a fine outcome. It just isn't the kind of subject *Good to Great* describes.

## 3. What the chair should hear

- Two Collins ideas transfer to any project and are worth using now. **Confront the brutal facts** (the Stockdale Paradox) and **keep a stop-doing list**. The facts below are urgent whatever framework is used.
- Three ideas don't transfer, and forcing them would mislead the commission:
  - A Hedgehog Concept, which needs years of iteration.
  - A flywheel, which needs repeated turns.
  - A BHAG, which needs a 10–30 year horizon.
- **For a fixed-scope, fixed-date project, "great" means delivering on time, on budget, without a deficit, and handing every legacy piece to an owner who outlasts the commission.** That standard is achievable. A Collins-style "great" is not.

## 4. Handover to the better-suited method

**Recommended method:** event and project risk management. That means a pre-mortem, a cash-flow stress test with go/no-go dates, a scope freeze, and a legacy handover plan. The facts below are a starting register for that work. They are not a Gate 4 output.

**Decisions the method should force, in order**

1. **Calder:** before the March deposit, get written reconfirmation of the $160,000 from Calder's current decision-maker. Set a go/no-go rule and a named fallback: rescope the tall ship or fireworks, ask other sponsors, or request a bridge from the town. Owner: chair plus treasurer `[ASSUMPTION]`.
2. **Re-base the budget** on a conservative attendance case. Rebuild ticket and merchandise projections from venue capacity and real comparables, not from 180,000. Owner: ED plus Program chair.
3. **Decide the app.** Either decline it, or cut it to the schedule and ticketing features that the 10-day festival actually needs. Fund the AR tour only if a permanent owner (library, school district or tourism office) commits to hosting and maintaining it. Owner: steering committee.
4. **Define legacy as handovers:** finish the remaining 99 oral-history interviews and transfer the archive to the library, deposit the time capsule with a named custodian, and secure performance rights for the symphony. Owner: Legacy chair.
5. **Write a one-page memo to the council about Bellmoor Days.** List what the commission will hand over (volunteer list, sponsor contacts, staging inventory) and let the council decide whether to charter a successor. Owner: chair. This stays outside the commission's scope.
