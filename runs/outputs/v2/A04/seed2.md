## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Kessling is no longer a small copy of the largest drug companies. It is the world's deepest specialist in a narrow field: medicines that act on hormones, inflammation and metabolism. That is the field where its steroid chemistry, low-cost fermentation process and early "firsts" already give it an edge. Most of its profit comes from compounds its own labs discovered. Licensed products fill gaps in its chosen fields and are never the backbone. Its clinical evidence is strong enough that regulators and specialists trust it before a product launches. It sells the seed business, or at least stops feeding it. Leaders are chosen for merit, family name or not, and the board can say no to them. The company beats the market for 15 years or more, measured by profit per research dollar rather than by research budget size.

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Stop acquiring non-drug businesses.** Freeze agricultural expansion now. Run a sale review of the seed business within 12 months.
2. **Stop benchmarking total research spend against the largest drug companies.** Benchmark spend *within the focus areas* instead.
3. **Stop funding research programs outside hormones, inflammation and metabolism.** Out-license or close them.
4. **Stop answering regulators' safety findings in court as the first move.** Answer with better data.
5. **Stop launching fixed-combination products** unless trials show each component adds benefit. (The withdrawn antibiotic was a combination product.)
6. **Stop in-licensing compounds outside the focus fields,** however attractive they look.
7. **Stop treating family membership as a qualification for CEO.**
