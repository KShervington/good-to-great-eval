## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Brennard is the supplier hospitals trust without a second thought. It sells the intravenous solutions, clinical nutrition, anesthetics and diagnostic systems that a hospital uses every day, and it designs each one to lower the total cost of diagnosis and treatment. It wins because its products are reliable and economical, not because of a lucky drug discovery or a consumer marketing budget. That returns the company to its founding idea: standardized, dependable medicine for doctors. Quality has the final say, as a function no sales target can override. Capital flows only to businesses that serve this purpose. Old patents and consumer brands are run for cash or sold. The company measures itself by how much profit each employee generates, so it gets leaner and more productive as it grows. It would survive the loss of any one product or executive, because the next sweetener ban or patent expiry would hit a single line rather than the core.
