## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Five or six years from now, Annika is the sonographer the region's adult congenital heart program depends on. She might be on the university's dedicated congenital team, or in a protected "complex and congenital imaging" seat at her current hospital. Her days hold fewer, harder studies: Fontan patients, repaired tetralogy, 3D datasets, and TEE support. That work uses her judgment more and her arm less. She owns the protocols other sonographers use. She teaches students whose curriculum includes congenital imaging because she wrote it. She is paid for what she knows, not for how many studies she gets through. Her shoulder is managed, not tolerated. The household has long-term disability cover and four or more months of runway, so no single injury or slow month for Marcus forces a bad decision. If she left, the clinic would lose something it could not replace for years.

**Non-negotiable disciplines:** Tuesday physio; an end-of-shift pain score logged daily; one congenital learning or visibility act a month (case review, protocol update, a talk); a quarterly money check with Marcus.

**Framework boundaries:** any new commitment must either raise the congenital share of her work or lower her hands-on hours per dollar. If it does neither, the answer is no, however good the money looks.
