## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Varden looks like the company that walked away from television and car radios: it leaves its own winning generation before anyone forces it to. In the great version, Varden's best engineers are leading the move to digital handsets and infrastructure on the standards carriers actually pick. The analog clamshell is treated as a cash-generating last chapter, not a flagship. Carriers see Varden as their partner for the transition, not as a supplier that rations hot phones in exchange for shelf space.
