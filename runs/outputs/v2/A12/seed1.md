## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Stop chain-wide deep discounting.** Price key items competitively in core markets, and only where cost savings fund it.
2. **Stop staying in markets it can't win.** Exit or sell regions where it can't reach #1 or #2 within ~5 years [ASSUMPTION: horizon].
3. **Stop paying out most of the profit.** Suspend or cut the dividend and reinvest in stores. The debt-free balance sheet can also carry modest borrowing.
4. **Stop running sub-scale, money-losing stores.** Close stores that store-level P&L shows to be unprofitable. Many will be under 8,000 sq ft.
5. **Stop cutting store labor in ways that empty shelves and lengthen lines.** Cut overhead, distribution and the store count, not the shopping trip.
6. **Stop letting the processing plant's volume needs shape the shelf.** Keep national brands in stock.
7. **Stop treating "largest grocer" as the goal.** Store count and total sales are not the scorecard.
8. **Stop treating the takeover defense as the strategy.** Avoid defensive acquisitions, special payouts and anything done mainly to beat the bid.
