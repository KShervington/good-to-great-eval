## 1. The great version in one paragraph

Great Lindqvist is a regional chain, not a national one. In each metro area it serves, it holds the #1 or #2 position. It got there by replacing small, outdated supermarkets with large combination stores where a household can buy the week's groceries, prescriptions, health and beauty items, and specialty foods in one trip. Its 40-year habit of treating purchases as votes becomes an advantage once electronic scanning reports those votes item by item, store by store, every day. The store brand, made in Lindqvist's own plants, earns trust through visibly honest quality rather than low price alone. It goes only to stores in markets Lindqvist leads, so the plants run full and distribution routes stay short. Lindqvist doesn't try to out-discount the largest rival everywhere. It uses the cost savings from density and scanning to price sharply where it leads, and it leaves markets where it can't lead. It grows store by store and market by market. Acquisitions are limited anyway, so growth comes from compounding the format and the data.

## 5. Stop-doing list

1. **Stop staying in markets where #1 or #2 isn't reachable.** Decide by rule, not by attrition.
2. **Stop investing in small conventional stores.** No remodels. Replace or close them.
3. **Stop new plant capital** (expansions, new facilities) until the store conversion program is funded. Plants exist to serve the store brand in markets Lindqvist leads.
4. **Stop expanding the family-center general-merchandise format** until its economics prove it beats combination stores per local population.
5. **Stop matching the largest rival's deep discounts everywhere.** Price sharply where Lindqvist leads, funded by density and scanning savings. Don't fight a national price war it can't win.
6. **Stop treating the drugstore and grocery divisions as separate growth stories.** One format, one flywheel.
7. **Stop running consumer research as a report.** It becomes the operating input for every conversion decision.

## 7. Core ideology and BHAG

**Core purpose:** *Make it easy for a household to get what it needs for its table and its medicine cabinet in one trusted trip.*

**Core values:**
1. **The shopper votes; we listen.** Purchases decide the stores, not executive taste.
2. **Honest product.** What the label says is what's in the package, especially in the store brand.
3. **Lead locally or leave.** Strength is measured market by market.
4. **Thrift with purpose.** Every dollar of capital pushes the store flywheel.

**BHAG (20-year horizon):** *Be #1 or #2 in every metro market we serve, with every store a combination store.*

**Clock-building mechanisms:**
- A written #1/#2 market rule with an annual review.
- A standard conversion playbook (format, departments, site criteria).
- Scanner and research data as a required input to every assortment and store decision.
- A store-brand quality audit independent of manufacturing.
- Succession: a grocery-operations deputy developed as a credible successor.
